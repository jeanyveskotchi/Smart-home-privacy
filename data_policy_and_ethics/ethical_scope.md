# Data Policy and Research Ethics

##  Privacy Principles
- All network captures are performed **within a controlled local environment** using personally owned devices.  
- No data is collected from other users, tenants, or public Wi-Fi networks.  
- The project follows the principle of **data minimization** — only traffic necessary for research is recorded.

---

##  Data Handling
- Packet captures (`.pcap` files) are **stored locally** and never shared publicly.  
- Any screenshots or examples shown online **exclude IP addresses** or device identifiers unrelated to the experiment.  
- Analysis focuses on **metadata**, not content or user communications.

---

##  Ethical Justification
This work aligns with academic goals in **cybersecurity education** and **privacy-preserving system design**.  
It demonstrates how even encrypted IoT systems may leak behavioral information — motivating stronger privacy controls.

All experiments comply with:
- **Institutional ethical standards** for non-invasive digital research.  
- **Responsible Disclosure Principles** — no exploitation, interference, or unauthorized access.

---

##  Researcher Responsibility
The researcher maintains full accountability for:
1. Securing all stored datasets.  
2. Respecting privacy boundaries of co-residents and networks.  
3. Ensuring any published results are anonymized and purely analytical.  

---

> **Summary:**  
> This research does not attempt to intercept or decrypt personal data.  
> Its sole objective is to understand and reduce privacy risks in smart home systems.
